This policy explains what the Shield Credit mobile app ("Shield Credit", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It matches the in-app Privacy Policy screen.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (handled by our authentication provider; we never see your password in plain text), and an optional name you enter at sign-up.
  • Documents you choose to scan: photos, images, or PDFs you upload, and text you paste. Uploaded files are stored privately under your account. Pasted text is processed but not stored as a file.
  • Content the app creates for you: scan summaries, possible issues, cases, notes, status history, letters and letter versions, and delivery records you enter (for example, that you mailed a letter on a date).
  • Settings and usage counts: in-app preferences and simple counts such as how many scans you ran (used for limits).
  • Server logs: event names (such as "scan completed"), your account ID, timestamps, and counts. Logs do not contain document contents.

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone audio, advertising ID, or device identifiers. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. How we use information

To provide the app: sign you in, store and show your documents and cases, analyze documents you submit, help you prepare letters, enforce usage limits, keep the service secure, and respond to support requests. We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. How documents are processed

  • PDFs are read on our own server.
  • Photos/images: when live analysis is enabled, our server sends the image to Google Cloud Vision to read the text.
  • Analysis: when live analysis is enabled, our server sends the extracted text (limited in length) or your pasted text to OpenAI to produce the summary and list of possible issues.
  • Letter help: only the short facts you confirm are sent to OpenAI, not the whole document.
  • We do not keep the full extracted text of a document after a scan; we keep the summary and a minimized result.
  • [OWNER CONFIRM: live analysis is currently turned off and the app uses built-in sample analysis. Update this note when you enable it.]

4. Service providers

We use these providers to run Shield Credit. They process data only on our behalf to provide their service:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and private file storage.
  • Render: hosting for our application server.
  • Google Cloud Vision: text recognition for document images (when enabled).
  • OpenAI: document analysis and letter drafting assistance (when enabled).

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of users or the service. [OWNER CONFIRM wording.]

5. Letters are never sent for you

Shield Credit does not email, mail, fax, or otherwise send letters to anyone. You decide whether and how to send them.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database access rules keep each account's records separate, uploaded files are stored in a private bucket, and server keys are never included in the app. No system is perfectly secure.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. You can permanently delete your account in the app at Settings > Privacy > Delete Account. This deletes your uploaded files, scans, cases, letters, notes, settings, usage records, and your sign-in account. You can also request deletion by emailing clb6577@gmail.com from your account email.
