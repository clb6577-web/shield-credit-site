Shield Credit Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Published by Clinton Brown, an individual based in Maryland, USA.

Contact: clb6577@gmail.com

This policy explains what the Shield Credit mobile app ("Shield Credit", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It matches the in-app Privacy Policy screen.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (handled by our authentication provider; we never see your password in plain text), and an optional name you enter at sign-up.
  • Documents you choose to scan: photos, images, or PDFs you upload, and text you paste. Uploaded files are stored privately under your account. Pasted text is processed but not stored as a file.
  • Content the app creates for you: scan summaries, possible issues, cases, notes, status history, letters and letter versions, and delivery records you enter (for example, that you mailed a letter on a date).
  • Short document excerpts: when you save a possible issue to a case, a short excerpt of the document text it refers to (up to 200 characters) is saved with it.
  • Settings and usage counts: in-app preferences and simple counts such as how many scans you ran (used for limits).
  • Server logs: our server's own logs record event names (such as "scan completed"), your account ID, timestamps, and counts. They do not contain document contents or IP addresses.

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone audio, advertising ID, or device identifiers. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. How we use information

To provide the app: sign you in, store and show your documents and cases, analyze documents you submit, help you prepare letters, enforce usage limits, keep the service secure, and respond to support requests. We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. How documents are processed

  • PDFs are read on our own server.
  • Photos/images: when live analysis is enabled, our server sends the image to Google Cloud Vision to read the text.
  • Analysis: when live analysis is enabled, our server sends the extracted text (limited in length) or your pasted text to OpenAI to produce the summary and list of possible issues.
  • Letter help: only the short facts you confirm are sent to OpenAI, not the whole document.
  • We do not keep the full extracted text of a document after a scan; we keep the summary, the possible issues, and their short excerpts.
  • Current status: live analysis is currently turned off. Scans use built-in sample analysis, the results are sample output and not an analysis of your document, and no document data is sent to Google Cloud Vision or OpenAI.

4. Service providers

We use these providers to run Shield Credit. They process data only on our behalf to provide their service:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and private file storage.
  • Render: hosting for our application server.
  • Google Cloud Vision: text recognition for document images (when enabled).
  • OpenAI: document analysis and letter drafting assistance (when enabled).

We share information outside these providers only when required by law, for example to comply with a valid subpoena or court order.

5. Logs and IP addresses

Our server uses your IP address only briefly, in memory, to limit how many requests can be made. It is not saved in our database or our logs. Our hosting and database providers (Render and Supabase) keep their own operational logs of requests to their systems, and those logs can include IP addresses.

6. Letters are never sent for you

Shield Credit does not email, mail, fax, or otherwise send letters to anyone. You decide whether and how to send them.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database access rules keep each account's records separate, uploaded files are stored in a private bucket, and server keys are never included in the app. No system is perfectly secure.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. The app does not delete data on a schedule. You can permanently delete your account in the app at Settings > Privacy > Delete Account. When the request completes, your uploaded files, your records in our database (scans, possible issues and excerpts, cases, letters, notes, settings, usage records), and your sign-in account are deleted. If you cannot use the app, email a deletion request to clb6577@gmail.com from your account email. We confirm the request with you at that address and aim to complete verified requests within 7 days (see Delete your account).

Our providers may keep backups and operational logs for a limited period set by their service plans. We cannot edit individual entries in them, and deleting your account does not remove copies that already exist there before they expire. Database backups do not contain your uploaded files.

9. Your choices

  • Export: Settings > Privacy > Export My Data gives you a copy of your data.
  • Delete: as described above.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete information. Contact us to make a request.

10. Children

Shield Credit is intended for adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, contact us so we can delete it. The app asks you to confirm that you are 18 or older before you continue.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version at https://clb6577-web.github.io/shield-credit-site/privacy.html with a revised effective date.
