Delete your Shield Credit account

You can permanently delete your Shield Credit account and its data at any time. This page applies to the Shield Credit app (package com.shieldcredit.app).

Delete in the app

  1. Open Shield Credit and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings > Privacy > Delete Account. (Delete Account is also listed on the main Settings screen.)
  3. Type DELETE in the box to enable the button.
  4. Tap Delete account…, then tap Confirm delete.

When deletion finishes, the app shows a confirmation and signs you out.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you want a copy of your data first, use Settings > Privacy > Export My Data before deleting.

What is deleted

  • Documents, photos, and PDFs you uploaded (removed from private storage)
  • Scans, scan summaries, possible issues, and their short document excerpts
  • Cases, case notes, and case history
  • Letters, letter versions, and letter delivery records you entered
  • Settings and preferences, usage records, and your profile
  • Your sign-in account (email and password login)

What may be kept

The items above are deleted when the request completes. Our providers (Supabase and Render) may keep backups and operational logs for a limited period set by their service plans. We cannot edit individual entries in them, and deleting your account does not remove copies that already exist there before they expire. Database backups do not contain your uploaded files. Our server's own logs contain event names, account IDs, timestamps, and counts, not document contents or IP addresses. Provider logs can include IP addresses.

Request deletion without the app

If you can't open the app or can't sign in, email clb6577@gmail.com from the email address on your Shield Credit account, with the subject "Shield Credit account deletion request". We will reply to that address to confirm the request before deleting anything, and we never ask for your password. We aim to complete verified requests within 7 days of receiving them, and we will email you when the deletion is done. The same items listed above are deleted.

Demo Mode

Demo Mode uses sample data stored only on your device. Choosing Delete Account in Demo Mode clears the demo session; no online account exists for it.
